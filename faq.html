FAQ

How much space do we need?

A flat spot about 3 metres by 5 metres, within 20 metres of where our van can park.

Do we need to bring anything?

Just swimwear and a towel. Firewood, kindling and a fire blanket come with every hire, and you can add our towel bundle.

How hot does it get?

Between 70 and 90 degrees. You can open the vent or the door to cool it down at any time.

Can we use it on the beach?

Yes, on any beach in Kestrel Bay where vehicles are allowed. We check the tide times with you before we come.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Saunas are made for cold, wet days. If there's a storm warning, we'll move your hire to another day for free.

How do we pay?

A 25% deposit books your date. The rest is due 14 days before your hire.
